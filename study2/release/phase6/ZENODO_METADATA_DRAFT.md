## Suggested description

This dataset contains the immutable Phase-6 source evidence for Study 2 (`S2-AEATR-001`) reported in the journal manuscript *Mission-Aware Satellite Cyber Response and Trusted Recovery Under Contact and Adversarial Evidence Constraints: Two Controlled Software-in-the-Loop Studies*. The controlled campaign contains 3,872 VALID observations, 0 INVALID attempts, and 85 frozen experimental cells. The evidence was generated in researcher-controlled software-in-the-loop conditions using synthetic identities, commands, mission states, evidence conditions, adversary states, and emulated contact behavior. The dataset does not contain operational spacecraft telemetry, production credentials, RF captures, classified/proprietary mission data, or evidence of testing against systems outside researcher control. Time values are deterministic logical SIL time, not spacecraft, network, or operator wall-clock latency.
