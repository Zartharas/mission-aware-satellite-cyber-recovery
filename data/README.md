# Local Data Directory

Raw third-party data and generated experiment data are intentionally excluded from Git.

`manifest.csv` is a **screening/rights register** for external datasets considered during research planning, calibration, benchmarking, or literature comparison. Its tracked columns are:

- source identifier and name;
- persistent identifier;
- directly verified or explicitly unverified license state;
- approved prospective use;
- whether raw data are committed; and
- current screening/status note.

The external datasets listed in this register are **not part of the frozen 720-observation statistical population** reported in Study 1. The reported primary analysis is based on the controlled WP9 campaign; raw WP9 evidence is archived in Zenodo v1.0.0 rather than under this directory.

A source may remain on hold when its file-level license has not been directly verified. A hold is not a submission blocker when that external dataset was not used as a Study-1 statistical input.

Do not place credentials, proprietary telemetry, operational packet captures, personally identifiable information, or unlicensed third-party raw data here.
