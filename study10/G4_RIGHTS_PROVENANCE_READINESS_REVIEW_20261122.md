## Reproducibility and integrity obligations

1. **Before any new original-source access:** Sole author explicitly confirms rights applicable to the exact release, intended local analysis, publication of derived non-raw outputs, required notices, and any third-party exclusions; preserve the record. No unsolicited third-party outreach is performed.
2. **After separate source-use authorization and at a controlled local environment only:** recompute SHA-256 and source-published MD5 for the six ESA ZIP archives (v2 main three; v1 sensitivity three), OPS-SAT v2 CSV pair and its LICENSE, comparing against the exact frozen manifest. Historical user-reported SHA-256s and outer ZIP-directory checks cannot be silently promoted to fresh internal verification.
3. **After authorization:** validate ZIP inner members via full CRC/decompression, detect unsafe paths and encrypted/special members, use bounded memory/storage/CPU/time and do not expand untrusted archives into shared or privileged directories. Do not interpret metadata differences as original telemetry payload difference without a separate content proof.
4. **Before native semantic adjudication:** capture file provenance, version, exact observation schema and operational decision-point evidence. Keep unknown distinct from false. Do not map CRC to signature, APID to trust, timestamp alone to freshness, anomaly label to security signal, or command presence to authorization.
5. **Before action analysis:** verify native decision time, evidence issue/validity, trust and epoch semantics. If absent, output `NOT_EVALUABLE_FOR_OPERATIONAL_ACTION`, not a fictional row-level rate. Do not count OPS-SAT or the three ESA strata as proven independent spacecraft replications.
6. **Before G4:** record a distinct sole-author G4 execution/source-use decision after rights/identity checks and (recommended) an immutable G3 evidence receipt. G2 and G3 approvals do not authorize G4. Record any necessary prospective protocol amendments rather than modifying the frozen G2 payload.
