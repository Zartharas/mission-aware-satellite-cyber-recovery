# AERC Deterministic Policy Engineering Probe

This app qualifies the pre-canonical shared snapshot and deterministic D0/D1 path.

It sends fixed engineering-only snapshots and verifies both the policy decision record and the recovery-sink record. It also sends malformed/invalid snapshots to confirm fail-closed behavior.

The probe does not execute a Study-7E scientific scenario, does not supply research-only truth, and does not claim that its fixed engineering actions are scientific outcomes.
