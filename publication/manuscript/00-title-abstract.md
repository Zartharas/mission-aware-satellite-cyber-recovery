# Mission-Aware Satellite Cyber Response and Trusted Recovery Under Contact and Adversarial Evidence Constraints: Two Controlled Software-in-the-Loop Studies

## Abstract

Satellite cyber response must contain unauthorized activity while preserving mission service and recovering only when current evidence supports a trusted state. We report two separately frozen software-in-the-loop studies using a NOS3/Fortytwo and cFS-based research environment. Study 1 comprised 24 cells and 720 VALID observations; Study 2 comprised 85 cells and 3,872 VALID observations and prospectively varied evidence compromise, contact, adversary budget, policy, and context ablations. The populations were analyzed separately without a weighted global policy score.

In Study 1, mission-state dependence was not demonstrated on predeclared primary outcomes. Modeled missed contact added approximately 10 s to ground-authorized containment, recovery, and state-divergence timing. Reduced policy-visible evidence changed the deterministic mission-aware selector from 30/30 trusted recoveries to 0/30 in the compromised-update block, and Pareto comparisons showed benefit, equivalence, and disadvantage.

In Study 2, bounded producer compromise exposed a stronger evidence-integrity boundary: fail-closed and evidence-aware policies achieved evidence-qualified recovery in all 96 paired primary observations while the adjudicated unsafe-permissive rate remained 1.0. Under modeled contact loss, the evidence-aware selector retained an unsafe-permissive rate of 0 by waiting for the next frozen contact opportunity, whereas the fail-closed baseline became unsafe-permissive under non-immediate contact. The benign/adversarial ambiguity block is interpreted only as a structural label-invariance control. Independent recomputation reproduced all Phase-7 numerical results with zero mismatches.

Together, the studies support condition-specific, evidence-explicit evaluation of satellite cyber response and trusted recovery rather than universal policy superiority. The findings are bounded to deterministic logical software-in-the-loop conditions and do not establish operational flight readiness, RF performance, or certification.
