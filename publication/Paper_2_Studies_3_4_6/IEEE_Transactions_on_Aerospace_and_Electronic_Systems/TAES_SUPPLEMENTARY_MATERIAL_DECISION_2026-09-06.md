# TAES Supplementary-Material Decision

**Paper:** Paper 2, Studies 3, 4, and 6  
**Journal:** IEEE Transactions on Aerospace and Electronic Systems  
**Decision date:** 2026-09-06  
**Status:** `FROZEN__NO_SEPARATE_SUPPLEMENTARY_UPLOAD_FOR_INITIAL_REVIEW`

## Decision

No separate supplementary-material ZIP, supplementary README, graphical abstract, or video abstract will be uploaded with the initial TAES Regular Paper submission unless the live Atypon ReX portal explicitly requires a file not identified in the public author instructions.

## Rationale

1. The 16-page manuscript is scientifically self-contained for peer review. The design, exact finite populations, principal endpoints, tables, residual-state identities, limitations, and cross-study synthesis needed to evaluate the claims are present in the manuscript itself.
2. The repository-bound study artifacts are reproducibility and provenance support. They are not required to understand the paper's methods or verify the manuscript's internal logic during ordinary peer review.
3. TAES public instructions state that electronic reference material should be supplied when it is essential to the reviewing process and otherwise unavailable to reviewers. No such unavailable essential material has been identified for this manuscript.
4. A separate supplementary package would duplicate repository evidence and create an additional version-control surface without adding necessary scientific content.
5. Study 3, Study 4, and Study 6 remain separately frozen. No supplementary package will be used to add new analyses, pooled results, reruns, post hoc experiments, or material that changes the manuscript's scientific identity.

## Repository availability

The research repository remains the controlled provenance source for the frozen studies and audit records:

`https://github.com/Zartharas/mission-aware-satellite-cyber-recovery`

If the TAES submission workflow presents a dedicated data/code availability field, the repository may be disclosed there using portal wording captured at submission time. This decision does not authorize inventing a portal field or attaching a redundant archive before such a field is presented.

## Reopen conditions

This decision may be reopened only if:

- the live TAES portal requires a supplementary file;
- an editor requests essential review material;
- a reviewer requires a specific frozen artifact unavailable through the repository; or
- a later accepted-paper production instruction requires a supplementary object.

Absent one of those conditions, the frozen initial-upload set contains the manuscript PDF only.
