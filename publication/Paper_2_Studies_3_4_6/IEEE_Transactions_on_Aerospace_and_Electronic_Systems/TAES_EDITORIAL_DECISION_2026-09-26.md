## Decision summary

The submission was examined by a panel of editors during pre-screening and was not sent to external peer review.

4. substantial prose emphasized scope limitations instead of the positive contribution;
5. the research question was difficult to understand and insufficiently connected to a clear research gap; and
6. the contribution statements emphasized experiments performed rather than meaningful findings for the aerospace community.
