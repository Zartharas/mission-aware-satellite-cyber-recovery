## 4. Originality and submission-history questions

TAES explicitly requires truthful handling of originality and prior review history.

Portal responses must be based on status at the moment of submission:

- manuscript is not concurrently under consideration elsewhere;
- manuscript has not been previously published;
- relevant prior author work is cited and the present contribution is clearly differentiated;
- if this or a substantially similar manuscript was previously reviewed and rejected by another journal, disclose that fact;
- if required, provide prior editorial correspondence and explain why the manuscript is being resubmitted.

Paper 1 at JAIS and Paper 4 at Acta are different manuscripts using different frozen study populations. They do not authorize any answer that conceals related prior work if a live portal field asks broadly about related manuscripts.
