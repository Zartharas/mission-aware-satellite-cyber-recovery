## Scientific role

The supplement preserves expanded material relocated from the frozen 16-page R9 article, including:

- Supplementary Fig. S1, the qualitative cross-study synthesis
- expanded Study-3 design, endpoints, treatment semantics, timing boundaries, and Table S1
- the complete Study-4 18-rule first/systematic threshold map in Table S2
- expanded Study-6 state and gate definitions and the complete gate frontier in Table S3
- expanded internal, construct, external, statistical, reproducibility, standards, and aerospace validity boundaries
- explicit frozen-population and non-pooling control

It does not add, rerun, enlarge, pool, or modify Studies 3, 4, or 6.

## Review-package rule

If the short-track package is adopted for TAES submission, the main 8-page article and this 12-page supplement are to be supplied together for peer review. The supplement is separate from the main article and does not increase the main article page count.

The main article remains independently interpretable, while the supplement provides the expanded deterministic-model detail needed for complete review and reproducibility.

## Claim controls

The supplement does not authorize:

- a combined Study-3 + Study-4 + Study-6 population
- a pooled success rate, p-value, confidence interval, common effect, or global policy rank
- interpreting Study-4 producer unavailability as contact loss
- interpreting Study-6 assurance-signal unavailability as contact loss
- converting Study-3 logical time into operational spacecraft time
- treating qualification as completed recovery
- claiming external replication
- claiming an integrated three-layer experiment
- claiming flightworthiness, mission availability, operational performance, standards compliance, or certification
