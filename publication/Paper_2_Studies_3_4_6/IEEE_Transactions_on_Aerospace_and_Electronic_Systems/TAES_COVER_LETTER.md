Editor-in-Chief

Dear Editor-in-Chief:

Please consider my manuscript, **“Residual Trust Boundaries in Satellite Cyber Recovery: Temporal Evidence, Producer Composition, and Artifact Assurance,”** for publication as a Regular Paper in *IEEE Transactions on Aerospace and Electronic Systems*. I am submitting the work as the sole author and corresponding author, and I am an independent researcher.

The manuscript examines residual trust boundaries in satellite cyber-recovery qualification through three separately designed, executed, and frozen deterministic studies. Study 3 evaluates temporal evidence qualification under continuous and synthetic intermittent contact. Study 4 maps multi-producer vote and provenance-domain qualification boundaries. Study 6 evaluates recovery-artifact assurance gates and residual incorrect states. The three study populations remain separate and are not pooled or represented as one integrated experiment.

The paper is aligned with the Aerospace Information Systems technical area because its central systems question concerns how recovery decisions are qualified from timing evidence, producer composition, provenance constraints, and software-supply-chain assurance. The manuscript emphasizes the remaining trust assumptions that are not directly observable to each gate, while preserving the limits of the finite models and avoiding flight-safety, mission-availability, or operational-probability claims that the experiments do not support.

This manuscript has not been published previously, is not under consideration by another journal, and has not previously been rejected by TAES or another journal. My separately submitted *Journal of Aerospace Information Systems* manuscript is a distinct publication based only on Studies 1 and 2; those study findings are not imported into this TAES manuscript.

The manuscript includes a transparent acknowledgment of substantive OpenAI ChatGPT (GPT-5.6 Sol) drafting and editorial assistance. The frozen experimental results were not generated or modified by the AI, and I independently reviewed and verified the text, citations, claims, calculations, and repository-bound evidence. Reproducibility and provenance materials supporting Studies 3, 4, and 6 are maintained in the public research repository: https://github.com/Zartharas/mission-aware-satellite-cyber-recovery.

Thank you for your consideration. I appreciate the opportunity to submit this work to *IEEE Transactions on Aerospace and Electronic Systems*.
