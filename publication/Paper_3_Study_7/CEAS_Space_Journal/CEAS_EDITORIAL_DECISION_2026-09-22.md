## Handling Editor comment

> "The manuscript addresses a relevant assurance concern and provides a transparent, reproducible experimental setup. However, the scientific contribution is insufficiently developed: the central finding follows directly from the problem formulation. Without a concrete spacecraft recovery architecture, validated trust assumptions, and comparisons with deterministic policies receiving the same information, the study remains an illustrative example with limited novelty and application-specific insight."

## Decision interpretation

The editor explicitly recognized two strengths:

1. the assurance concern is relevant; and
2. the experimental setup is transparent and reproducible.

The rejection is therefore recorded as a publication-contribution and application-specificity rejection, not as a finding that the frozen Study-7 execution is irreproducible or internally invalid.

The editor identified four scientific-development deficiencies:

1. the central result is too directly implied by the problem formulation;
2. the manuscript lacks a concrete spacecraft recovery architecture;
3. the trust/independence assumptions are not validated;
4. the corroboration comparison lacks a deterministic policy receiving the same information as the learned corroboration-aware policy.
