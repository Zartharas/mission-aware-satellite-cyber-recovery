# Official Elsevier declaration-tool — author-completion handoff (not tool output)

For IJCIP, the author must use Elsevier's **Declaration of Interests** tool and upload its generated Word file even where there is nothing to disclose. The tool requires email-based one-time-passcode access; this note is **not a replacement, imitation or valid upload**.

1. Open https://declarations.elsevier.com/ or follow the Declaration tool link in the IJCIP Editorial Manager upload screen.
2. Enter the published corresponding email `asingh65430@ucumberlands.edu` and complete the one-time-passcode login.
3. Select the reported work (no external research funding stated in prior Paper 3 materials); check any financial/non-financial support carefully.
4. Review **outside relevant support over the previous three years**, including current employment, consulting, stock/equity, honoraria, grants, sponsored collaborations and professional affiliations. Do **not** automatically mark none based solely on the manuscript; examine whether any organization could be viewed as relevant.
5. Review patents, copyrights and other related intellectual-property interests; disclose anything relevant. Original research copyright alone may or may not require a specific entry, depending on the tool.
6. Review other activities, editorial/journal roles and relevant nonfinancial interests.
7. If after reviewing these categories there are no relevant interests, select the tool's `I have nothing to declare` option and download its generated `.docx`.
8. Inspect the tool-generated content against the manuscript's funding/competing-interest statements; do **not** edit the tool-generated file. Upload that exact document under the journal's Declaration of Competing Interests file type.
