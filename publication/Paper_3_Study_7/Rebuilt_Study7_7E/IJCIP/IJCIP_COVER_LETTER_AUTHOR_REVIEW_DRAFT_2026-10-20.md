# IJCIP cover letter — author-review draft (not sent)

Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript, **“Architecture-Grounded Trust Separation for Satellite Cyber-Recovery Decisions in Space-Enabled Critical Infrastructure,”** as a full research article for the *International Journal of Critical Infrastructure Protection*.

Space-enabled services support communications and other critical-infrastructure operations. This manuscript examines a specific assurance problem: whether apparently valid evidence is sufficient to authorize a cyber-recovery action when the evidence paths may share compromised trust domains. The study focuses on recovery-decision correctness rather than anomaly detection accuracy.

The manuscript brings together two explicitly separate, frozen experimental studies. Study 7 establishes an information-sufficiency boundary for deterministic and learned recovery selectors. The prospectively designed Study 7E extension adds a cFS-grounded reference architecture, five modeled trust-domain dimensions, and equal-information deterministic and learned comparators. Its 196 held-out scenarios yield 784 policy decisions. Corroboration reduces unsafe proceeds for the deterministic comparison but increases them for the learned comparison, with corresponding opposite changes in false-conservative holds. Adverse-transfer cases are retained. These findings argue for assessing recovery decision logic together with the architecture generating its evidence.

The contribution aligns with the journal’s interest in scientific and engineering approaches to critical-infrastructure protection. It does not assert deployed spacecraft validation, flight qualification, or measured real-world safety or outage probabilities. The manuscript includes full methods, frozen evidence provenance, study limitations, and AI-use disclosures.

**Author confirmation before sending:** The work is original; this manuscript is not under consideration by another journal; all necessary rights and permissions have been obtained; related manuscripts and previously presented portions have been appropriately disclosed. Paper 3 uses Study 7 and Study 7E observations only and does not reuse other papers’ result populations.

Thank you for considering this work.
