# Cover letter

Dear Editor,

I submit my sole-author research manuscript, **“Architecture-Grounded Trust Separation for Satellite Cyber-Recovery Decisions in Space-Enabled Critical Infrastructure,”** for consideration as a Research Paper in the *International Journal of Critical Infrastructure Protection*.

Space-enabled communications and other critical infrastructures depend on recovery decisions made from evidence that may originate in compromised or correlated trust domains. My study asks when apparently current, authenticated and corroborated evidence is insufficient to authorize spacecraft cyber-recovery action, and how deterministic and learned recovery selectors respond to the same information.

The manuscript reports two separately frozen experimental studies, without pooling their populations. Study 7 establishes an information-sufficiency limit across 1,033 modeled observations. Study 7E extends the investigation through a cFS-grounded reference architecture, five modeled trust-domain dimensions, and matched-input deterministic/learned comparators tested over 196 held-out scenarios (784 policy decisions). Corroboration does not yield uniform improvement: deterministic and learned selectors show different unsafe-proceed/false-conservative-hold trade-offs. Adverse-transfer cases and null controls are retained.

The article's contribution is a bounded recovery-authorization assurance result, not a new anomaly detector, an onboard learned-policy deployment, a flight-qualified system or an estimate of real-world cross-sector outages. The scientific methods, freeze identifiers, original data provenance and limitations are documented, and the research, figure-code and manuscript-preparation uses of AI assistance are disclosed.

An earlier **Study 7-only** version of the Paper 3 research was submitted to *CEAS Space Journal* and was rejected without publication. The present manuscript is a rebuilt and materially extended **Study 7 + Study 7E** article, not concurrently submitted to CEAS. This research is distinct from the other manuscripts in my satellite cybersecurity publication program (Papers 1, 2, 4 and 5), and their experimental populations have not been imported. Full related-work citations and data provenance are provided.

No funding was received for this work. I report no relevant competing financial or non-financial interests, subject to the accompanying publisher-generated declaration.
