# Prospective Paper 5 robustness extension — concept only, NOT FROZEN OR AUTHORIZED

## Scientific motivation
The third-round assessment identified a construct-induced all-unknown result and a full unconstrained Boolean cube. Stronger novelty requires evidence that the admission method discriminates legitimate decision evidence from tempting proxies and that feasible-state dependencies are grounded in a defined architecture. This proposal does not change either Study 9 or Study 10, retroactively amend their protocols, or claim new results.

## Candidate design (requires author design approval and separate freeze)
1. **Positive/negative semantic controls:** choose a documented recovery interface independent of the current results; define decision-time authority, explicit evidence semantics, and independently justified expected mappings before creating or inspecting the held-out controls. Include valid direct evidence, valid predeclared derivations, misleading CRC/identifier/timestamp/label proxies, missing timing/authorization, contradictory and incomplete evidence. Use synthetic source records clearly labeled as synthetic; do not treat them as real-flight observations. Predeclare ground truth from specifications, not from the adjudicator's answer. Separate fixture creator and checker logic where feasible for one author; report lack of human inter-rater evidence.
2. **Constrained-state sensitivity:** before any selector execution, derive joint feasibility restrictions from an explicitly documented recovery architecture, not from desired smaller sidecars. Keep the original full cube as frozen comparator. Predeclare feasibility conditions, nonempty domain, known/unknown distinction, policy set, admissible completion rule, and reporting of action-set inclusion/minimal sidecars. Prove each restriction is warranted or mark it hypothetical. Report negative outcomes and invalid constraints; do not interpret conditional mathematics as flight performance.
3. **Optional source-level extension:** separately authorize complete v1 semantic re-adjudication and/or Hack-A-Sat secondary contrast only after version/rights and granularity checks. Freeze the original broader RQ endpoints and comparison units before inspecting additional results; avoid pooling. If necessary collect authoritative decision-time witness data via a lawful controlled lab in a distinct study, not retrofitting real-flight claims.
4. **Stop rules:** no real source data without rights/provenance; no operational authorization claim from synthetic inputs; no changing frozen S9/S10 files. Keep new output paths, manifest and prereview distinct. Predeclare rejection/falsification criteria; any post-freeze change must be a disclosed deviation.
