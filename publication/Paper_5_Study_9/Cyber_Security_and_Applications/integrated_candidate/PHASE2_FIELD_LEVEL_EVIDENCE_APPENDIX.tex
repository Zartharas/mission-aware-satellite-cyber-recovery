\section{Field-level evidence and decision-time admission}
\label{app:fieldledger}
The following is an editorial presentation of the frozen source-to-predicate judgments, not a new adjudication. Each row is one required recovery predicate in one frozen dataset or published-schema stratum. The machine-readable original rationales and provenance remain in the repository files \url{study9/results/S9-RTSI-001_CANONICAL_RUN_20260914/mapping_matrix.json} and \url{study10_derived/S10_G4_TierA_32Slot_PublishedSchema.json}. Field candidates are listed because they were considered, \emph{not} because they were accepted as decision-time recovery evidence. A missing candidate in this table does not prove the underlying mission lacks the capability. In the tables, A means \texttt{AMBIGUOUS}, X means \texttt{ABSENT}; Study 10 evidence-role suffixes are M (experiment metadata only), G (offline ground truth only), and D (documentation only). No row was direct or predeclared-derivable.
