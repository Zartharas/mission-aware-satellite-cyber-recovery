# Cover Letter Draft

Dear Editor-in-Chief,

Please consider the manuscript **"Recovery-State Semantic Interoperability and Decision Identifiability Across Public Space-Cyber Datasets"** for publication as a **Research Article** in *Cyber Security and Applications*.

This paper examines whether independently produced public space-cyber datasets expose the semantic state required by a fixed downstream recovery interface. It uses a preregistered field-level mapping rubric, preserves unavailable recovery state as unknown, and evaluates downstream action identifiability exhaustively rather than imputing missing mission, trust, freshness, contradiction, or authorization semantics.

Across CuCD-ID v3, AegisSat 2025, and UNSW-IoTSAT 2026, direct and direct-or-derivable recovery-state coverage are both 0/8 under the frozen semantic contract. None of the four frozen recovery policies yields a uniquely identifiable action from native dataset state alone, and six to eight additional sidecar variables are required to guarantee a unique action depending on policy.

The paper does not claim that the datasets are low quality or that semantic interoperability, intrusion response, or cyber recovery are individually new. Its contribution is the narrower combination of field-level recovery-state mapping, exhaustive action-identifiability analysis, and minimal-sidecar derivation across independently sourced public space-cyber datasets.
