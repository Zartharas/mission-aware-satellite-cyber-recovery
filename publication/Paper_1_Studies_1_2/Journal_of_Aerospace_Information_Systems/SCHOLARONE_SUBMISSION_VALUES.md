**Plain Language Summary:**

Satellites may need to respond to cyber incidents when communication with the ground is delayed or when security evidence is incomplete or misleading. Using controlled software simulations, these studies show that response and recovery outcomes depend on the quality and trustworthiness of available evidence, authorization, contact opportunities, and the policy being used. No single policy performed best in every tested condition.
