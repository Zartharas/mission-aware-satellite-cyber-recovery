## Capture rule

For every live field, record:

- exact field label;
- exact help text when material;
- required versus optional status;
- allowed values/dropdown choices;
- Paper-1 proposed response;
- evidence screenshot or portal note where useful;
- whether the response is scientific, administrative, legal/rights, or editorial metadata.

Do not enter an institutional-review identifier, grant number, reviewer identity, conflict, classification authority, postal code, or other factual value unless it is actually supported by the research record or supplied/approved by the author.
