# Artificial-Intelligence Disclosure — AIAA / JAIS

**Status:** target-specific disclosure draft based on current public AIAA ethics guidance verified 2026-09-04. Exact ScholarOne field wording remains submission-workflow dependent.

## Proposed in-manuscript disclosure

During preparation of this journal work, the author used **OpenAI ChatGPT** to assist with manuscript organization, source checking, editorial refinement, consistency review, reproducibility documentation, repository and audit workflow support, and preparation of journal-submission materials. The author reviewed and edited all resulting content, checked scientific quantities and source claims against the frozen research record and cited sources, and takes full responsibility for the manuscript.

For Study 1, ChatGPT-assisted work occurred after the experimental campaign and historical statistical findings were frozen and after the Study-1 evidence package had been archived. It assisted with reconstructing, reviewing, and testing a public statistical reproducibility implementation from preserved derived inputs, outputs, and provenance records. It did not generate campaign observations, consume campaign seeds, alter the 720-observation statistical population, change exclusions, or change historical statistical outputs.

For Study 2, ChatGPT-assisted workflow support occurred after the campaign evidence and prospective analysis implementation were frozen. It assisted with execution/review of the hash-bound analysis workflow, independent audit and reproduction support, claim-boundary review, responsible-release documentation, and journal integration. It did not generate or replace observations, alter seeds, change exclusions, modify the frozen primary analyzer, or provide input to the evaluated response policies.

The evaluated Study-1 and Study-2 response mechanisms are frozen deterministic rule-based software mechanisms and do not use generative artificial intelligence or machine learning as the scientific response method.

## Placement rule

Current AIAA policy also requires a brief description of qualifying artificial-intelligence use in the technical paper, for example in acknowledgments or footnotes. The authoritative manuscript already contains a detailed declaration under its declarations section. Before final JAIS export, confirm whether retaining that dedicated declaration satisfies the live AIAA template/editorial workflow or whether a brief acknowledgment/footnote cross-reference is also required.

## Scientific boundary

Artificial-intelligence assistance is a publication/reproducibility tooling disclosure. It must never be represented as:

- an experimental treatment;
- an onboard or ground response policy;
- a learned autonomy mechanism;
- a source of frozen observations;
- a replacement for independent statistical verification;
- evidence of operational flight autonomy.
