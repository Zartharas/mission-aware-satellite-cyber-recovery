# Artificial Intelligence Disclosure - AIAA / JAIS

**Status:** target-specific disclosure draft based on current AIAA ethics guidance verified 2026-09-04. Exact ScholarOne field wording remains submission-workflow dependent.

## Proposed in-manuscript disclosure

During preparation of this article, I used **OpenAI ChatGPT** for editorial and research-support tasks, including manuscript organization, source checking, language refinement, consistency review, reproducibility documentation, repository and audit workflow support, and preparation of submission materials. I reviewed and revised the resulting text, checked scientific quantities and source claims against the frozen research record and cited sources, and take full responsibility for the manuscript.

For Study 1, this assistance was used only after the experimental campaign and historical statistical findings had been frozen and after the Study 1 evidence package had been archived. It supported reconstruction, review, and testing of a public statistical reproducibility implementation from preserved derived inputs, outputs, and provenance records. It did not generate campaign observations, consume campaign seeds, alter the 720-observation statistical population, change exclusions, or change the historical statistical outputs.

For Study 2, this assistance was used only after the campaign evidence and prospective analysis implementation had been frozen. It supported execution and review of the hash-bound analysis workflow, independent reproduction and audit work, claim-boundary review, responsible-release documentation, and journal integration. It did not generate or replace observations, alter seeds or exclusions, modify the frozen primary analyzer, or provide input to the evaluated response policies.

The Study 1 and Study 2 response mechanisms are frozen deterministic rule-based software mechanisms. Generative artificial intelligence and machine learning are not part of the scientific response method evaluated in either study.

## ScholarOne disclosure rule

Current AIAA policy requires qualifying artificial-intelligence use in manuscript preparation to be disclosed during submission in ScholarOne. The exact live question and response options must be copied from ScholarOne and answered as presented.

## Placement rule

Current AIAA policy also requires a brief description of qualifying artificial-intelligence use in the technical paper when such tools were used for writing or permitted figure preparation. The JAIS manuscript already contains a disclosure in its declarations section. The live ScholarOne wording remains authoritative for the portal response.

## Scientific boundary

Artificial-intelligence assistance is disclosed as publication and reproducibility support. It must not be represented as:

- an experimental treatment;
- an onboard or ground response policy;
- a learned autonomy mechanism;
- a source of frozen observations;
- a replacement for independent statistical verification;
- evidence of operational flight autonomy.

## Current policy authority

AIAA, *Ethical Standards for Publication of Aeronautics and Astronautics Research*, section addressing use of artificial intelligence in AIAA publications. Live policy remains authoritative on the submission date.
