## Live sources checked

### Computers & Security scope

Official Elsevier journal page / shop entry:

- `https://shop.elsevier.com/journals/computers-and-security/0167-4048`

Current scope language still states that cryptology has been excluded as a principal component since 2006 and that, from early 2024, submissions featuring AI or ML as significant components are under a moratorium. This is the principal scope-sensitive boundary for Paper 1.

### Elsevier generative-AI journal policy

- `https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/generative-ai-policies-for-journals`

Current policy requires transparency for substantive AI-assisted manuscript preparation, including a disclosure statement upon submission. It also states that AI use in the research process, including code development or data analysis, should be described in the methodology when applicable. AI tools must not be credited as authors. Human authors remain responsible for accuracy and integrity.

### Elsevier highlights guidance

- `https://www.elsevier.com/researcher/author/tools-and-resources/highlights`

Current general guidance:

- 3–5 bullets;
- 85 characters or fewer including spaces;
- avoid jargon/acronyms where possible;
- highlights are not part of editorial consideration;
- highlights are generally supplied at final-files stage as a Word document.

The current repository highlights satisfy the 3–5 and <=85-character conditions.

### Elsevier Your Paper Your Way guidance

- `https://www.elsevier.com/subject/next/guide-for-authors`

Current general guidance indicates that initial submissions have no strict formatting requirement if the manuscript contains the essential scientific elements. DOI use is encouraged, and exact journal reference formatting can be applied later when the journal uses Your Paper Your Way.
