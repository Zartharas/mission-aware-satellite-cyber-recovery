## Generative AI policy

Elsevier's journal policy was updated in June 2026. Substantive use of generative AI or AI-assisted technologies in manuscript preparation requires a separate declaration statement in the manuscript immediately before the references. The statement should identify the tool/service, explain the purpose of use, and confirm author review, editing, and responsibility. Basic spelling, grammar, and punctuation checks do not require disclosure.

If AI tools were used as part of the research process rather than manuscript preparation, that use belongs in the Methods section. No such research-process AI use should be invented.

Official policy:

`https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/generative-ai-policies-for-journals`
