## 4. Resolved conflicts

### 4.1 Free Format versus IJSCCN house style

Free Format means initial submission does not require exact final styling. It does not negate required content, limits, artwork rules, GTOC, biography/photo, DAS, or journal-specific file requirements.

**R5 decision:** use a polished single-column Word manuscript and proactively adopt IJSCCN numeric references, but do not imitate final Wiley production layout.

### 4.2 Generic Wiley figure guidance versus IJSCCN figure guidance

Generic Wiley guidance prefers PDF for some line art and commonly cites 300 to 600 dpi. IJSCCN specifically requests TIFF or EPS and specifies 800 dpi preferred, 600 dpi minimum for graphs and drawings.

**R5 decision:** IJSCCN controls. Use TIFF/EPS, regenerate line art from source at 800 dpi where technically appropriate, and never raster-upsample.

### 4.3 Tables in Free Format versus IJSCCN table placement

Free Format permits flexible initial formatting, but IJSCCN states that tables must be on separate pages after the reference list.

**R5 decision:** follow the stricter journal-specific placement in the initial R5 manuscript.

### 4.4 Data Availability Statement in manuscript versus portal

Wiley submission help may collect a DAS directly in the portal, while IJSCCN and Wiley data policy require the statement as part of the article record.

**R5 decision:** include a concise DAS in the manuscript and use substantively identical wording in the portal field when presented.

### 4.5 Generic AI disclosure placement versus live portal requirement

Wiley permits AI disclosure in Methods, Acknowledgments, or an equivalent location depending on use. The author's live IJSCCN/Wiley workflow explicitly required the disclosure within Methods.

**R5 decision:** put the substantive disclosure in Methods. Avoid relying on a Declarations-only placement.
