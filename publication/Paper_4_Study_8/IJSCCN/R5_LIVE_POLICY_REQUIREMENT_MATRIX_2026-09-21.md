## 3. Resolved ambiguities

### 3.1 Literal IMRaD headings

**Evidence:** IJSCCN requires the scholarly components, but current Original Papers use field-specific section names.

**Decision:** method and result content must be unmistakable, but literal “Methods” and “Results” headings are a design choice rather than a hard requirement.

### 3.2 Initial tables

**Evidence:** Free Format allows text, figures, and tables together or separately. The same journal page's style section asks for tables after References, and revision makes that placement explicit.

**Decision:** R5 will use separate-page tables after References because it is the stricter and revision-ready interpretation. Classification: conservative initial-submission design choice, mandatory at revision.

### 3.3 Initial figures

**Evidence:** Free Format permits embedded initial figures, while the journal's illustration section strongly directs separate TIFF/EPS files and revision makes separate files mandatory.

**Decision:** produce separate publication-quality TIFF files for R5 and ensure the main reviewer manuscript remains understandable.

### 3.4 Figure legends

**Evidence:** legends are required; generic Wiley recommends a dedicated post-References legends section.

**Decision:** use a dedicated legends section as a professional R5 design choice.

### 3.5 DAS placement

**Evidence:** Wiley Authors help says the DAS is entered directly in the portal and need not be in the main manuscript, while Wiley data guidance and IJSCCN call for a DAS as part of the article/manuscript record.

**Decision:** include it in the manuscript and enter matching portal text.

### 3.6 AI disclosure placement

**Evidence:** Wiley requires disclosure of substantial AI assistance but permits context-appropriate placement. The author's live Research Exchange workflow required disclosure within Methods.

**Decision:** Methods controls for Paper 4.

### 3.7 Paper 4 versus Paper 5

**Evidence:** Wiley explicitly warns against duplicate/redundant and fragmented publication.

**Decision:** non-overlap is a formal submission-integrity gate. Study 9 / Paper 5 evidence must not enter Paper 4.
