# Cover Letter

Dear Editors,

Please consider the manuscript **"Post-Quantum Trusted Recovery Under Intermittent Connectivity: Feasibility Across Modeled Contact Budgets and Public Observation-Opportunity Timing"** for publication as an Original Paper in the *International Journal of Satellite Communications and Networking*.

The manuscript addresses trusted cryptographic recovery for satellite systems when communication opportunity is intermittent and post-quantum transition objects impose substantial transfer burden. It reports two separately governed studies that are presented together without statistical pooling. Study 8 evaluates a complete deterministic population of 3,456 logical-contact positions under fixed modeled capacity. Study 8E applies the same frozen transition requirements to prospectively governed public SatNOGS observation-opportunity timing and solves the minimum hypothetical uniform effective payload rate required for modeled trust restoration before elapsed-time horizons.

The primary policy finding is deliberately bounded. The added contact-aware pre-commit guard does not improve the respective feasibility endpoint over ordinary staged cutover in either frozen evidence layer. The results instead show how transition-object burden, communication-opportunity timing, and recovery horizon constrain modeled recovery, while policy semantics redistribute security-state and availability costs.

The work is directly aligned with the journal's focus on satellite communications, satellite systems, networking, protocols, and performance-related systems analysis. SatNOGS observations are used only as observation-opportunity timing proxies. The manuscript does not claim authenticated command contact, measured link throughput, onboard post-quantum execution performance, or operational spacecraft recovery validation.

This work is original, has not been published elsewhere, and is not under consideration by another journal. The author reports no external funding and no competing interests. Supporting data, code, frozen protocols, result manifests, and audit records are publicly archived. The manuscript includes a Data Availability Statement and a transparent Methods disclosure describing the use of OpenAI ChatGPT / GPT-5.6 Sol for manuscript-development assistance. The author independently verified the submitted content and retains full responsibility for it.
