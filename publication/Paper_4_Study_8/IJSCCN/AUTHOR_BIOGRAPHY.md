# Author Biography

**Aman Kumar Singh, MS, DSc** is an independent cybersecurity researcher based in The Woodlands, Texas, United States. His research focuses on satellite cybersecurity, trusted cyber recovery, cryptographic agility, mission-aware security decision systems, and reproducible security engineering for space and other intermittently connected systems. His recent work emphasizes evidence-qualified recovery, post-compromise trust restoration, post-quantum transition constraints, and explicit claim boundaries between modeled analysis and operational validation. He is the sole author of the present study and is responsible for its conception, methodology, software, validation, analysis, data curation, writing, visualization, and project administration.
